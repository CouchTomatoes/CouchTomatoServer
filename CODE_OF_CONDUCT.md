# Code of conduct

CouchTomato follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity. We want issues,
pull requests and discussions to be friendly and useful.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take criticism of the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting links to pirated content, or asking others to share it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break these
rules. To report a problem privately, use
[a private report](https://github.com/CouchTomatoes/CouchTomatoServer/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.
